## 1. Executive Summary

This revised term sheet sets out the proposed terms for the full intellectual-property assignment of **AuditChain Ledger** (versions v1.0.0 through v5.0.0) as currently delivered in the public monorepo.

AuditChain is a progressive, pure-Python cryptographic audit platform that demonstrates:

- Immutable hash-chained and Merkle-based audit trails
- Multi-tenant isolation and educational zero-knowledge range proofs
- Simplified Raft-style consensus, sharding and auto-healing
- Educational lattice-based signatures, Shamir threshold sharing, AI compliance scoring and cross-partition receipts

The package is positioned as a **high-quality reference implementation and R&D acceleration asset**, not as a finished, NIST-validated production product.

- Immediate access to a clean, progressive architecture spanning classical audit trails through educational post-quantum primitives.
- Zero external cryptographic dependencies — suitable for air-gapped and sovereign environments.
- Documented threat model and known limitations reduce diligence risk.

- Acceleration of internal R&D (estimated 4–9 months of specialized engineering effort avoided, depending on target maturity).
